1 · The real-world problem

After a death, subscriptions keep charging while the account balance sits frozen

When a parent or partner passes away, their recurring PayPal payments do not stop automatically. Streaming services, cloud storage, and gym memberships keep pulling money every month. Meanwhile, the family has no single screen to see what is still running, cannot easily reclaim charges that happened after the date of death, and has to email every company one by one during grief.

2 · How Heirloom works

One guided portal for the family’s chosen executor

Heirloom is an Estate Resolution Portal. The person who uses it is the executor — typically a son, daughter, or surviving partner who was pre-authorized by the account holder while they were still alive (just like Apple’s “Legacy Contact” or Google’s “Inactive Account Manager”).

5 · Plain-English tech glossary

Tools we use (in case you haven’t used them before)

PayPal Sandbox

PayPal’s official test environment (`api-m.sandbox.paypal.com`). It runs the exact same APIs as real PayPal — subscriptions, orders, captures, refunds, payouts — but uses fake test accounts and fake euros. Our test business account currently holds 5,007.59 EUR.

Order vs. Capture vs. Refund

In PayPal, an Order is a checkout request. When the buyer pays, PayPal creates a Capture (the actual completed charge with its own ID). To return money after death, you call `/refund` on the Capture ID.

AG Grid & AG Studio

AG Grid is an industrial-strength interactive table library for React. AG Studio is its dashboard/reporting layer with a built-in AI chat sidebar. We forked PayPal’s official Next.js + AG Studio starter template so all the table plumbing is already wired.

Zero-Click Rehearsal Mode

Normally, cancelling a PayPal subscription destroys it forever, requiring manual browser clicks to approve a new one. We built a rehearsal mode that suspends subscriptions instead of cancelling them, plus a 1-command script (`reset-estate.mjs`) that wakes them back up and creates a fresh charge using a PayPal test Visa card — zero browser clicks needed.
